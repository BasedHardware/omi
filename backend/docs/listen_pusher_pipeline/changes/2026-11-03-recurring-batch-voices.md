# Private recurring voices at batch finalization

The successful, revalidated batch whole pass can now retain sealed unknown-voice
centroids under existing private-cloud and other-voice consent. Thirty seconds
of owner-disjoint evidence and two distinct conversations are required before a
profile groups future batch speech. Joint person/profile arbitration abstains
on ambiguity; automatic detection never creates or names a person.

Existing identify cards offer trusted transcript mentions and voice near-misses.
A manual assignment may bind a profile to a current person print within .35.
Rejections tombstone profiles; conflicting assignments retract and unbind.
Conversation/person deletion, privacy opt-out and account wipe remove authority
and evidence. No live roster, streaming GPU or past-conversation refresh changes.

Defaults on inside batch final pass; rollback with
`SPEAKER_RECURRING_VOICES_ENABLED=false`. The lead owns TTL setup and cohort ramp.
See ADR-0008 and the recurring-voices section of
`backend/docs/runbooks/batch-conversation-speakers.md` for retention and metrics.
