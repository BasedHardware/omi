# Fence episode finalization against deletion

Python rechecks deletion authority inside the episode finalization transactions described in the [episode lifecycle](../../listen_pusher_pipeline.mdx#episode-lifecycle-and-overdue-quiet-recovery). This covers transcript-tail moment commits, episode closes, episode updates including discarded-row reconciliation, recorded sync attachment after a memo miss, sync rejoin that reopens an episode, and finalized attach memos. An active or malformed account deletion marker or any conversation tombstone aborts the transaction before it stages writes. The shared account policy permits `cancelled` and `billing_failed` markers, with the conversation tombstone still checked.

The change ships with the Python backend and has no new flag. Revert the PR to roll back. The fence adds up to two reads per transaction attempt and adds zero writes and zero provider calls. An update and memo that each run once add four reads. A memo retry after transcript preload adds two more, and paths that also commit a tail or close an episode each add their own reads. Recorded attachment and reopen also contribute reads under the episode update fence site (`episode_finalization_update_fence_account` and `_tombstone`).

Regression tests insert each marker after planning and verify that stored episode, membership and memo state stays intact. They also verify the two allowed account marker statuses with and without a conversation tombstone. R19 review, serving verification and production read-count readback are tracked in SCA-4083. The [PR](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/pull/26190) documents the metric and the rollout readback procedure.

Each fence site reports its two reads separately in `omi_firestore_document_reads_by_site_total`: `<site>_account` for the account deletion marker and `<site>_tombstone` for the conversation tombstone. The four unsplit site values stop being emitted from the deploy that carries this split, so a readback on the old selectors shows absent data, not a quiet fence. `_account` `hit` means a marker document exists when the fence decided: marked, including the allowed `cancelled` and `billing_failed` statuses. `miss` means unmarked. Refusals are not counted directly. Every attempt that passes the account check reads the tombstone, so blocked-account refusals equal `_account{outcome="hit"}` minus `_tombstone` attempts on marked accounts, and tombstone refusals are `_tombstone{outcome="hit"}`. A tombstone refusal on an unmarked account appears as `_account` `miss` followed by `_tombstone` `hit`. Reads, writes and decisions are unchanged.
