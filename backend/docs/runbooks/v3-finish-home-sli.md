## DESIGN

SCA-4489 measures the v3 TestFlight Finish cohort for SCA-4434.

- Start at the deliberate cut admitted by the capture controller, before its WAL drain. Have the pendant double-tap and app Finish call sites pass an explicit, bounded source. Carry one opaque attempt token and the cut time through the existing request retry. Use the existing v3 admission and TestFlight distribution signals. Preserve eligible older-build traffic in an unknown-source bucket where a server-side cut can identify it, and report its timing basis explicitly. Keep temporarily unbound and empty Finishes in the denominator. Capture attempts that never reach the Finish API through the existing authenticated client telemetry transport if the cohort is to include local-only cuts.
- Bind the attempt at the existing manual-end transition. Cover the current recording and no-pointer window/recording fallbacks. Reuse the idempotent cut and source lineage to correlate with the derived window, including when the cut consumes a previous finish request and when two consecutive Finishes share a capture session. Keep already-committed retry handling idempotent. Classify held when the deliberate cut enters a predecessor hold, fast when the winning transcript comes from fast Finish, batch when ordinary batch wins, and unknown when there is no route evidence. Held takes precedence.
- Reuse first durable summary and the Home visibility predicates as the readiness inputs. Validate the candidate against TestFlight's `GET /v1/home/feed` and its legacy conversations fallback, including the versioned Home projection and pending/hidden/merged row exclusions. Use the timestamp at which the summary-bearing card becomes available to the reader. The first client poll is useful validation evidence and would include polling and app foreground time if used as the SLI endpoint.
- Join content-free observations in read-only production telemetry. Keep user IDs, raw recording IDs, and all content out of events and metrics. Use a per-Finish opaque join key only in restricted events, and bounded route, source, and outcome in aggregate output. Gate collection independently with a default-off kill switch and preserve capture, retention, privacy custody, and Home behavior.
- For UTC cut times in `[cohort_start, cohort_start + 24h)`, dedupe by Finish and left-join each to its first qualifying summary. Read after the final cut has had at least 60 seconds plus ingestion delay. Report totals, at-or-under-60s, late, unmatched, and unknown by route and source. Report matched-only p95 with coverage and the all-Finish p95 only when identifiable. Report the serving revision and bounds.
- Verify both Finish sources, request retry and cross-worker duplicate events, empty/no-pointer paths, two successive cuts, held/fast/batch route attribution, summaryless and hidden Home rows, projection lag, and the kill switch. Run focused backend checks with a bounded timeout via `omi-heavy`.
