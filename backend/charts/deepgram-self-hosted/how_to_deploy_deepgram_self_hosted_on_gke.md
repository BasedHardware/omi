# Self-hosted Deepgram retired

Omi deployment overlays and installation instructions are retired. The vendored
Nova charts remain archival source, not an Omi deployment target. Do not reinstall
them. Cloud Deepgram and BYOK routing remain supported.

Use [the audio fleet runbook](../../docs/runbooks/audio-fleet.md#self-hosted-deepgram-retirement)
for the post-deployment caller proof and lead-owned resource teardown. Zero replicas
alone do not prove there are no callers.
