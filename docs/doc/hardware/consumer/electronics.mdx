## Board Overview

The Omi uses three interconnected PCBs:

| Board | Function | Latest Version |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Mainboard** | nRF5340 SoC, nRF7002 Wi-Fi, microphones, IMU, flash, battery management | v1.2 |
| **Charger Board** | Magnetic dock with pogo pins, USB-C power, ESD protection | v1.0 |
| **FPC** | Flexible cable connecting mainboard to charging contacts | v1.0 |

## How to Order PCBs

<Warning>
The mainboard is an **HDI (High-Density Interconnect) PCB** with WLCSP components at 0.35mm pitch. It requires an HDI-capable PCB fabricator and PCBA assembler with: blind/buried via capability, via-in-pad (filled and capped), controlled-impedance stackup, fine stencil (0.075mm), and X-ray inspection. Many low-cost PCB services will reject this board or silently alter the stackup. The charger board and FPC are simpler and can be ordered from standard PCB services.
</Warning>

Upload the Gerber ZIP along with the BOM CSV and CPL (pick-and-place) CSV to your PCB manufacturer. Use the exact specs below per board:

| Parameter | Mainboard | Charger Board | FPC |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Layers | 4 (F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, B.Cu) | 2 | 2 |
| Material | FR4 | FR4 | Polyimide (flex) |
| Thickness | 0.6mm | 1.0mm | 0.3mm |
| Board Size | 21.0 x 21.0 mm | 13.0 x 20.0 mm | 29.3 x 12.5 mm |
| Surface Finish | ENIG (immersion gold) | ENIG | ENIG |
| Min Trace/Space | 0.076mm / 0.076mm (3 mil) | 0.2mm / 0.127mm | 0.254mm / 0.1mm |
| Solder Mask | Both sides | Both sides | Both sides |
| Copper Weight | 1 oz outer, 1/3 oz inner | 1 oz | 1 oz |

<Warning>
The mainboard has blind vias (L1-L2, L3-L4) and buried vias (L2-L3) — confirm your fab supports these before ordering. Total 414 drill hits including 159 blind + 55 buried vias.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Use **ENIG** surface finish for all boards. HASL causes bridging on the nRF5340 and nRF7002 fine-pitch pads (0.35mm ball pitch).
</Tip>

## First Power-On Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visual Inspection">
    Inspect visible passives and connectors for solder bridges, tombstones, and missing parts under a microscope or loupe. **WLCSP joints (nRF5340, nRF7002) are hidden under the package** — visual inspection cannot verify them. Request X-ray inspection from your assembler, or proceed to electrical bring-up and diagnose from there. Check orientation of polarized capacitors and ICs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power Rails">
    Connect bench supply (set to 3.7V, current limit 100mA) to VBAT (TP9, bottom side) and GND (TP12, top side or TP18, bottom side). Measure:
    - **VBAT** at TP9: 3.7V (battery input)
    - **VDD_3V3** at U16 output: 3.3V (nRF5340 I/O rail)
    - **VDD (nRF5340 core regulator decoupling):** ~1.0V at the DEC1–DEC4 decoupling capacitors near the nRF5340 (these are the internal regulator outputs, not directly measurable at a pin — check with a fine probe on the decoupling caps)

    <Warning>If current draw exceeds 50mA before firmware flash, disconnect immediately — likely a short. Do not connect a bench supply while a battery or USB charger is also connected unless you understand the BQ25101 power path.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flash Firmware">
    Connect SWD debugger (J-Link) to test points: SWDIO→TP3, SWDCLK→TP8, GND→TP18, VTref→VDD_3V3 rail (sense input, not power — J-Link uses this to set I/O voltage levels), optional RESET→TP14 (all bottom side except TP12). Flash network core first, then application core. See [SWD debug guide](https://github.com/BasedHardware/omi/blob/main/omi/hardware/consumer/electrical/SWD-DEBUG-ACCESS.md) and [firmware guide](/doc/developer/firmware/Compile_firmware).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify BLE">
    Open nRF Connect mobile app. The device should appear advertising as "Omi". If not, check 32MHz crystal and antenna matching network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Board doesn't power on" icon="bolt">
    Check BQ25101 output (3.7-4.2V). Verify TPS628438 buck converter has correct input. Inspect visible solder joints under microscope; WLCSP joints require X-ray or electrical bring-up checks. Measure resistance between VBAT and GND — should be more than 1kΩ before power.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No BLE advertising" icon="bluetooth">
    Verify 32MHz crystal is soldered (not just tacked). Check 32.768kHz crystal for RTC. Measure nRF5340 VDDH (should be 3.3V) and DEC capacitor voltage (~1.0V). **Do not modify antenna trace or matching network** (L3, L4, C12, C15) — these are tuned for the production enclosure. If using a different enclosure, expect detuning and plan for VNA measurement and re-matching.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No Wi-Fi scan results" icon="wifi">
    nRF7002 requires its own 40MHz crystal. Check BUCKA/BUCKB rails. Verify SPI connection between nRF5340 and nRF7002. Wi-Fi requires firmware support enabled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Poor or no audio" icon="microphone">
    TDK T5838 (MMICT5838-00-012) microphones are **bottom-port** per TDK distributor data — the sound inlet is on the underside of the component facing the PCB. Sound enters through an acoustic hole in the PCB beneath the mic. Verify:
    - The PCB acoustic holes under MIC1/MIC2 are clear (not blocked by solder paste, adhesive, or debris)
    - The enclosure acoustic holes align with the PCB acoustic vias so the sound path is unobstructed
    - Dust filter (5.8×4.0×0.1mm, BOM item 74) covers the enclosure acoustic holes but does not block the sound path
    - PDM clock (1–3.25MHz) and data lines are connected correctly
    - MIC1 and MIC2 are in the correct PDM L/R channels (check CLK polarity)
    - No flux residue or solder paste in the microphone sound port
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Charging doesn't work" icon="battery-half">
    Check pogo pin contact resistance (less than 0.5 ohm). Verify FPC connection. Measure charger board USB-C VBUS (5V). Check TPS25200 eFuse output. Verify magnet polarity alignment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
