Omi is an open-source AI wearable. A small necklace device captures your conversations, the Omi app transcribes them in real time, and Omi extracts memories, action items, and summaries you can search and chat with afterwards.

Everything that makes it work — mobile apps, backend, firmware, and hardware designs — is open source in this repository.
