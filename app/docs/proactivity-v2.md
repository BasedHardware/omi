# Mobile Proactivity v2

`HomeDailyRecaps` keeps Daily Recaps and mounts `HomeForYou` beneath it. Pull-to-refresh reloads both. The optional For You section uses generated proactivity DTOs through `backend/http/proactivity.dart`; empty, disabled, malformed and failed feed reads hide it. It displays the first populated feed page, scanning at most ten empty candidate pages. Cards use the same neutral styling for every producer.

`VisibilityDetector` reports `shown` only for a visible card on the current route while the app is active. Account-scoped local receipts prevent repeated exposure events through refresh and restart. These receipts expire after 31 days, beyond the feed's 30-day item lifetime. A display timeout never produces an outcome.

`HomeNavigation.openRoute` and `openHomeDeepLink` return whether the destination successfully opened. Task resolution uses its canonical by-ID read and edit sheet; conversations use their canonical detail route. Missing targets return false. The outcome follows successful opening, and an account epoch fences delayed fetches, navigation and callbacks.

`ProactivityPush` recognizes `notification_type=proactivity_v2` with `item_id`, `target_kind` and `target_id`. FCM background/terminated taps and Awesome Notifications taps converge through `NotificationUtil.handleFcmDataTap`. Mentor v2's allowlisted `/chat/mentor` override is preserved. Legacy plugin pushes keep their existing dispatcher. Cold-start taps wait for the normal authenticated owner binding before navigation. Opening records `channel=push`; receiving a push does not record `shown`.

The outbox stores up to 100 unsent generated outcome requests, UUIDs, exposure receipts and producer opt-outs, with no feed content or credentials. Transport failures, retryable auth failures, 429 and 5xx retain the same event ID. A 30-second timer, app resume and feed refresh retry; Retry-After takes precedence. Terminal failures are dropped. Storage writes serialize before transport. Sign-out and account switch clear receipts, optimistic state and pending events, and every transport attempt checks its captured account epoch after header building. At capacity, further receipts are refused until space is available.

Analytics uses `AnalyticsManager.proactivityOutcome` with only action, channel and surface. Analytics failure cannot stop outcome persistence. Positive/negative aggregation and producer disable authority remain server-owned.

Hermetic tests: `test/unit/proactivity/`, `test/widgets/home/home_for_you_test.dart`, and the canonical navigation/notification regression suites. Synthetic widget screenshots are generated by the local, ignored `app/.local/pv2_render_test.dart`; PNGs stay outside Git in the run's `renders/` directory. Device and served-backend acceptance remain coordinator release checks.
